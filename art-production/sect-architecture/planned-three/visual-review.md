# Visual findings: Citadel, Sky Monastery, Iron Foundry

This joint review contains **three distinct architectural identities** and nine real PixelLab sprites. It is an early art milestone. The PNGs, prompts, generation receipts, source masters, shared 32-colour candidate palettes, native previews and technical checks are included. The user's complete briefs for IDs 0–2 are in `../design-registry.json` and `../references/sect-descriptions-ids-0-5-partial.txt`.

- **Citadel:** The hall reads as a towered stone fortress, and the separate gatehouse reads as a guarded entrance. The first mountain was a square stone block and was rejected; its raw PNG remains in `iterations/`. The corrected mountain has a more irregular ridge, but it still contains artificial platform-like edges. The hall also includes a grassy/path foundation baked into its sprite. Neither mountain has been fitted to the castle or connected to roads and walls. The portcullis is visually suggested, not functional.
- **Sky Monastery:** Curved turquoise roofs and a layered hall silhouette distinguish it in colour and grayscale. The peak and bridge are separate PNGs. The peak is too narrow and has a conspicuous squared top; it does not yet express several naturally fractured summits. Bridge endpoints and high-level paths are unmeasured, and the waterfall, other peaks, residences and bell tower are absent.
- **Iron Foundry:** The dark hall, furnace tower and smaller workshop establish a soot-and-fire material family. The furnace opening reads at close zoom. The workshop is still too similar to a reduced forge hall and needs stronger weapons-making details; the mechanical hammer is not clear enough. Ore mountains, coal yards, cooling basin, miners' quarters and wide cart roads are absent.

The [hall silhouette comparison](previews/silhouette-comparison.png) shows three different roof and tower profiles without colour. In the [isolated v79 browser test at 16px per terrain tile](previews/v79-in-memory-16px.png), the three identities remain separable mainly by their overall shapes and values; details such as masonry, roof trim and iron equipment compress heavily. The [32px-per-tile capture](previews/v79-in-memory-32px.png) makes the architectural vocabulary clearer. These are in-memory visual probes only. The mountains and bridge are deliberately not installed in the game because the current renderer lacks measured attachments and elevated navigation.

Technical validation passes for all nine **candidate canvases**: readable transparent PNGs, binary alpha, a shared 32-colour candidate palette per family, no resampling, and exact preservation of each source alpha shape. This is not artistic approval. No physical route, anchor contact, collision, growth stage, two meaningful variants per building role, or complete sect pack has been validated. The existing v79 game, published settings and saves are unchanged.

The batch used **ten free PixelLab daily generations**: three halls, six support sprites and one Citadel mountain correction. The account reported **$0.00 credits** and no purchase was made. Current free generation balance is zero.
