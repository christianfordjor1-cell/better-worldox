# Three provisional PixelLab sect art identities

The user asked to see at least three sect designs together before the next artwork review. The three queued identities are Citadel (ID 0), Sky Monastery (ID 1) and Iron Foundry (ID 2). Their complete descriptions are preserved in `../design-registry.json` and `../references/sect-descriptions-ids-0-5-partial.txt`.

The [visual overview](previews/overview.png) contains nine actual PixelLab-generated transparent PNGs: a principal hall and two independent supporting objects for each of the three identities. The [native and 3× review sheet](previews/native-and-3x.png) shows every source at its actual pixel resolution beside its adapted review canvas. The [technical report](review-report.md), [visual findings](visual-review.md), [manifest](manifest.json) and [isolated v79 browser probe](browser-probe.json) document what passed and what still needs artwork.

Each principal hall was generated at 144 × 144 pixels using the established shallow three-quarter view and upper-left lighting. The exact PixelLab inputs are under `requests/`; completed outputs remain untouched in `masters/`. A first Citadel mountain iteration was rejected as too block-like and is preserved in `iterations/` next to the corrected candidate. `../tools/run_planned_three.py` and `../tools/run_planned_supports.py` checked the free generation allowance before submitting batches and did not purchase credits. Palette mapping generated one shared 32-colour candidate palette per sect, retained all source alpha pixels and used no resampling.

**These nine PNGs do not complete any sect.** Before a full architecture pack is claimed, each sect still needs independent residences, specialty buildings, entrances, landmark layers, navigation parts, at least two meaningful architectural variations, growth stages, anchors and real in-game visual checks. This is a joint early visual review of three identities. Some shapes remain artistically weak and are identified in `visual-review.md`.

The three older 48 × 48 PixelLab account objects were inspected. The industrial and medieval miniatures lack the scale and separate construction details required here; two medieval images are effectively duplicates. They are references, not substitutes for the queued artwork.
