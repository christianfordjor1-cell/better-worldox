# Remaining production work

Continue from the preserved v79 game and this rendering adapter. Keep gameplay definitions, world generation, identity fields, saves and import/export compatible. All tooling remains free: Blender, Python/Pillow, browser Canvas and LibreSprite where available. No purchased assets, paid tools or outsourcing are required by this roadmap.

1. **Review the playable integration.** Check the actual world, portraits, scale, animation readability and render performance with the provisional PNGs. Keep the original-art toggle and fallback until replacement artwork has passed visual review. Improve confirmed integration defects without rebuilding working gameplay.
2. **Produce deliberately authored anatomical artwork.** Use the approved Phase 1 designs and isolated Blender warrior as references. Build species-appropriate silhouettes, orthographic controlled-palette renders and pixel-level cleanup. Deliver separate usable parts with shared 144 × 144 cells, anchor (72,130), matching poses and tested sockets. The current preposed body sheets cannot be converted into convincing interchangeable anatomy by metadata alone.
3. **Expand by verified body plans.** Preserve all 19 race contracts and 17 beast contracts. Validate clothing, age, build, equipment attachments and colour masks for bipeds, quadrupeds, birds, snakes, spiders and dragons. Author convincing walk/slither/flight and action poses, including extra limbs, tails, wings, horns, muzzles, beaks, fur, feathers and scales. Three-quarter mirroring remains the current directional limit.
4. **Retain complete identity and progression coverage.** Preserve existing appearance ranges, seeded identities, independent colour combinations, baby/adult and child/adult/elder forms, beast patterns and ancient bloodlines. Keep all 15 classes, 48 specialisations including six ranger specialisations, equipment, 30 elemental roots, six cultivation branches and supported progression. Extend art beyond the pack's six visual tier rows without changing the game's nine-stage progression rules.
5. **Verify missing gameplay definitions before adding them.** Current art-only species are tiger, snake, eagle, spider and crow; additional race artwork does not create additional mechanical races. Obtain review of their intended gameplay definitions before introducing new ecology, stats, progression or movement rules. Existing beastmaster, summoner and wildlife systems stay in place.
6. **Complete the distinct-sect design registry.** Preserve the exact 30 architectural labels and procedural definitions in [sect-registry.json](sect-registry.json). Verify the intended correspondence to previously designed sect types before filling missing meanings. Develop genuinely distinctive buildings, roofs, robes, philosophy, vegetation, reachable mountain layouts and seeded growth from approved definitions. The separate 30-root registry must never substitute for sect types.

Each production increment needs actual rendered evidence, compatibility checks, game-world visual inspection, bounded-memory/performance checks and save round trips. Mark assets as approved production, provisional working, legacy or missing; technical slot coverage is not finished artwork. Keep Blender assets separate until fit, pose, palette and anchor compatibility are demonstrated.
